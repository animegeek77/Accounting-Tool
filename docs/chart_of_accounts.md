1. Project & Reporting Rules

All income and cost accounts (4000–5999) are project-taggable.
Operating expenses may optionally be allocated to projects.
Client advance receipts are never treated as income until earned.
Pass-through costs must net to zero profit unless explicitly marked otherwise.

1. Structural Rules

Account codes are immutable once created.
Account types are immutable.
New accounts must follow numbering conventions.
Deprecated accounts may be archived but not deleted
